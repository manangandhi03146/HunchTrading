# hunch frontend redesign

Scope: developer console, reusable prediction widget, racing and esports demos. Mode: Operate. The user explicitly delegated direction selection: “Choose the direction you think fits hunch best.” Build path: code-led. Working behavior and the existing collaboration changes remain authoritative.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the lifecycle of a prediction market easy to operate. Replace the tiny purple card grid with a readable workspace and aligned application and market rows.

OWN-WORLD: Oslo festival program, translated into conventional application controls. Deep moss navigation, ivory work areas, orange actions, Manrope type, simple rules and restrained corners. No decorative timetable costume.

STORY: Developers see current market states and pooled test tokens, create a market, inspect settlement events and configure the embeddable widget. Mock data is explicitly identified.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 248px moss navigation rail beside an ivory workspace. A clear page title and create action lead into a compact state strip; application pools and a dark integration panel sit above the market table. On mobile the rail becomes a wrapped navigation header. Signature interaction: choosing an outcome changes its rule, checkmark and pool emphasis together; the racing lifecycle remains visible and functional.

FORM: Grounded candidate 6 of 7; seed a09cead0. Remaining candidates were federation bulletin, cycling route cards, civic wayfinding, Bauhaus study sheets, magazine colophon and industrial catalog. Restrained color strategy suits developers working indoors under typical office lighting.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
